## 2. Purpose and judging fit (PRD 4.1)

Meetings and follow-ups are the Future of Work gap; this makes them a measured, governed flow. Technical Merit (40%): reuses harness, Verifier and outbox; every extracted item cites transcript spans and code checks it; identity, dates, the wake gate and the assistant's facts are code, not model; the analyst has no tools (16.3); a Google-only pipeline (Gemini Live, Calendar, Gmail, Model Armor, Cloud Tasks, Firestore, Cloud Run). Problem Alignment (25%): a new time-saved unit `meeting_minuted` against a hand-written baseline (EV-G2). Innovation (25%) and UX (10%): people join the world as actors; meetings, mail, tasks, pins and calendar meet in one plate and one side panel, identical on every channel. Delete-the-model test (4.2): minutes, drafting and spoken answers break without the model; plate, reminders, the wake gate and navigation rules are code on purpose (PR-9), so the Personal Agent costs nothing while idle. Candidate scenario SC-10 "Meeting to follow-up" becomes config-only afterwards (4.3).

## 4. Cost design

- **Dev and CI:** fake providers, recorded answers, synthetic transcripts and calendars (example.com); no `LIVE=1`. The user records answers once; proposed cap 20 analysis calls and 1 live voice session.
- **Analysis:** transcript chunked by time, lowest-cost profile first, escalate on schema failure or Verifier rejection with the reason logged (EFF-02); evidence, dates, identity and the wake gate are code; one summary call. Proposed target: a 60-minute transcript inside <= 100 credits (US$0.50), calibrated on the first recording.
- **Gemini Live (FR-MTG-16), cap proposed for the user's sign-off:** <= 3 credits per meeting minute averaged (US$0.015, US$0.90 an hour, the upper end of the meet-bot estimate for addressed-turn audio) and <= 120 credits per meeting as a hard cap, reserved by the Treasury (14.1); at the cap the bot falls back to chat-only replies, then silence. Cheapest path: always-on ears are streaming speech-to-text gated by a voice-activity detector (5 to 15% of audio is sent, measured); a Live session opens only when the wake gate fires, takes the agenda and minutes so far as a short text card rather than audio history, and closes when the attention window ends. Prices are unset in `config/prices.yaml`; the caps are in credits so they hold when the user sets them.
- **Assistant panel (FR-PA-19):** rules answer >= 80% of questions with no model call; the rest use the cheapest profile with the caps in FR-PA-19; caching on record fingerprints.
- **Calendar (FR-CAL-08):** free API, sync tokens, working-window polling, zero model calls to render.
- **Personal Agent:** no always-on model; sweeper, plate and reminders are code and templates; nudges go to the panel and one email digest a day per person.
- **Face (FR-MTG-14):** one canvas at 320x240, <= 15 fps, no model, no extra service.

## 6. Consent, privacy and safety (rules PE-01 to PE-21 in `contracts/people.rules.md`)

1. **Who may record:** a signed-in Owner, in a meeting they host or attend, after a web attestation. Test meetings only, no confidential employer or client data (PRD 2.4).
2. **Disclosure and opt-out:** fixed, versioned text from config (AI notetaker, 30-day transcript, who sees it, `Tilo leave` stops it), posted in chat before capture and spoken when voice is on. Any participant can end it; default is discard.
3. **Retention and visibility:** no raw audio stored; transcript 30 days, minutes and items 90 days; deletion within 30 days of account deletion (16.5). Transcript and minutes are owner-only; an assignee sees their item, the meeting title and one evidence quote; sharing minutes is a T3 send.
4. **No monitoring (23.5):** no talk time, sentiment, ranking or activity score; no view of another person's plate; team data is only what people choose to put on the team board or calendar.
5. **Follow-ups on someone's behalf:** a reminder to oneself is a notification. Any message to another person goes draft (T1), card, `approval.resolved`, outbox, with `authz` on `data.transfer`. A new email, an invite or a calendar entry with attendees is T3 (BQ-171): L1 only, PIN on WhatsApp (SEC-06). No autonomous follow-up in this release.
6. **Injection:** transcript, Meet chat, calendar text and pin titles are untrusted data. Model Armor screens first; the analyst has no tools and returns structured data to code; flagged content marks the record and makes any draft T3 with `safety_flag`. An item changes only by its owner or the meeting Owner, never by words in a transcript. The assistant model sees read-model rows as delimited data and can emit only a validated `ui_action`.
7. **Google data** stays under Limited Use (16.6). Calendar stores only title, times, all-day flag and attendee count (PE-13).
