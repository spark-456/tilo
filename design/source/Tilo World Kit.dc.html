The world, as a system
Everything on this canvas is drawn by one renderer from a plan. Nothing is hand-placed. District blocks are sized from their buildings. Blocks sit on a grid, the gaps between them become roads, and agents stand at doors or walk real routes along those roads. Change the plan and a different town comes out.
Projection
True isometric at 30°. One light: top faces lightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest. Flat fills, no gradients or blur.
Grid
48-unit tile. Roads are 1.05 tiles wide. Buildings are inset 5 units from their cell, and a lane in front of each row is where agents stand.
Budget
11 common buildings, 4 footprints × 4 floor counts × 3 roofs × 3 facades, 1 agent rig, 6 accessories, 8 faces, 6 road pieces.

How the generator works
1 · Pack. Each district's buildings are packed into rows of up to 7 tiles. The block is sized to fit, with a lane in front of every row.
2 · Arrange. Town Hall takes the centre slot of a 3×3 grid. Other districts fill slots in a fixed order, and empty rows and columns collapse.
3 · Roads. Every gap between blocks, plus a ring road, becomes road. Handoff routes use doors, lanes, road rows and the nearest cross street.
4 · Style. The planner names a generated building and gives it an icon. If it leaves out floors, roof or facade, the renderer picks them from a hash of the name. Shuffle changes the seed; a given seed always gives the same town.
5 · Honest motion. Only three things move: dashed routes (walking, from real routes), bobbing walkers, and pins for work in progress.

Rules that keep any plan looking good
Colour is reserved. Tints appear only on roofs, plots and signs. Amber, red and green appear only on pins, rings and gates.
One loud thing. Needs-you gets a flag pin plus an amber ring on the ground around the agent. Nothing else is saturated at that size.
Height budget. The renderer caps floors at 4 and at 2 for footprints of 2×2 or more, so tall buildings never hide a row.
Back to front. Districts fill slots behind or beside Town Hall first, so the most active work sits where the camera sees it.
Access. A locked district still takes up its space, so the layout doesn't shift when access changes, but it is drawn only as clouds and a lock. No partial data.
Performance. Every part is a flat polygon with no filters. A 40-agent City is about 2,400 SVG nodes. Canvas or WebGL instancing takes over above that.
