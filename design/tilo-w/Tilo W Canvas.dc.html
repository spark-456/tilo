BUDGETTransform and opacity only, so the compositor does the work. No blur, no filter animation, shadows only on floating cards. At most one walk, one talk loop and one tool bob at once on a typical run.
HIDDEN = PAUSEDAn IntersectionObserver and visibilitychange set data-paused on the World root. tilo-w.css then pauses every animation underneath, and the replay timer stops.
REDUCED MOTIONprefers-reduced-motion (or the reduced prop) turns every move into a 200 ms fade. Agents appear where they are, loops show one still frame, and the parcel and confetti are skipped.
