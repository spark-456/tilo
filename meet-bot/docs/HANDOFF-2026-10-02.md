## What the live meetings showed (2026-10-01 evening, all with the user)
- The **Express key is heavily throttled**: the same tiny call took 0.7 s, 1.3 s, 5.5 s, 14.8 s, and once 429 after 12.6 s; answers through `quick_reply` took 11 to 18 s from the host. `models.get` and `models.list` return 401 for API keys (prewarm now turns itself off). This is the root of the old "12 s model calls". The code cannot fix it; a free **AI Studio key** (aistudio.google.com/apikey, plain `genai.Client(api_key=...)`, not `vertexai=True`) is the likely fix and needs a Studio backend ticket (below) plus the user adding the key.
- **Speech recognition is poor on the user's mic**: Scribe and Meet's own captions both turned "tell me a joke" into "tell me, John" / "tell me your joy". Mitigated by CV-02, not solved.
- **First sound is slow even for instant answers**: 5.3 s and 9.0 s after the question. The local espeak voice renders the whole text before playing; the ElevenLabs free plan refuses the default library voice (`paid_plan_required`), and that first failing call cost about 8 s (now remembered between meetings). The user needs `ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID` of a voice they own for ElevenLabs to work.
- The `audit` command agreed with the logged first-sample mark to about 0.1 s on a real run.
- `mic_turn_off` and `account_button` selector misses at join are harmless. The Meet admit pill click occasionally times out (the pill is gone by click time when the guest withdraws); admit retries every 3 s.
