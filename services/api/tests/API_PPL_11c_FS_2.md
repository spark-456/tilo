# PPL-11c-FS-2 (a)+(b): write limits, idempotency records and event writes on durable stores

Tickets: FS-2 (a) store-backed `Limits` and `Idempotency-Key` records, (b) the work-item event writer. Not covered: (c) plate sources, source owners, Desk and meeting worlds (ADR-0026's world store).
Tests: `test_PPL_11c_FS_2_{limit,key,store,event,emu}_*.py`, guards `test_PPL_11c_FS_2_guard_*.py`; helpers `_ppl11c_fs2_lib.py`, `_ppl11c_fs2_fake.py` (a Firestore stand-in with serializable transactions, preconditions, field transforms, an outage lever and a lost-answer lever). No contract change, no new event type.

Rows `[firestore]` of `limit` 01, 04 to 09, and `limit` 11 and 12, `key` 01 `[firestore]` and 07, `store` 06 and `event` 03 also pass today (the in-memory limiter keyed by the stores object behaves the same on one instance; a dead event writer is already the fixed 503); they are pinned in `FS2_GREEN_TODAY` and stay green. Today: 40 pass, 47 strict xfail (`--runxfail` shows them failing for the missing feature), 7 emulator tests skip. Run the emulator rows with `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` set by whoever owns the emulator.
- Seam: the limiter of a `PeopleStores` built by `firestore_people_stores(client)` or `open_people_stores(env, client_factory=)` is `people_limits.limits_of(stores)`, and for Firestore stores it is backed by that same client (one `firestore_client()` in prod.py feeds both; no second client, no second composition root). `Limits()` with no arguments stays the in-memory limiter. The org of every limiter document is `deps.ORG_ID`. A store failure inside `write_limit` or `idempotent` leaves as `HTTPException(503, "unavailable")`.
- `Limits.erase_person(org_id, user_id) -> int` is the FR-MEM-09 hook (documents deleted).

## 1. `limit` (SEC-10; BQ-PPL11C-2, -13)
01 the 61st POST attempt in a minute is 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After` 1 to 60; reads never counted; another principal free; a minute later free again (memory and firestore).
02 two instances share one count (30 + 30 over alternating instances, then 429 on both). 03 a restart forgets nothing.
04 the window slides (30 at t=0, 30 at t=59, at t=61 exactly 30 more). 05 a 413 is the 60th attempt. 06 a refused attempt is not counted (60 refusals do not extend the wait). 07 `Retry-After` is whole seconds to the oldest counted attempt (40 at t=20; 1 at t=59). 08 every people POST route shares one count.
09 property (Hypothesis, 220 steps, 3 principals): accepted exactly when fewer than 60 accepted ones are under a minute old; the cap never exceeded; `Retry-After` matches the model.
10 the limiter uses the one client the stores were built with (factory called once, no `firestore.Client(...)` of its own). 11 two installs, two databases: no module state. 12 four racing threads directly on the limiter: at most 60 accepted. 13 four racing threads over HTTP on two instances: at most 60 accepted, a 503 is the fixed body.

## 2. `key` (PE-19; BQ-PPL11C-4, -14)
01 a replay is the first answer byte for byte, nothing written, no event (meeting, work item, person; memory and firestore; on firestore no write to a people record collection). 02 the same on another instance. 03 another body is 422 `idempotency_key_reused` on every instance. 04 a key never crosses principal or route, across instances (no 422 oracle). 05 a record is a replay for 24 h of the request clock (23 h 59 min yes; 24 h 1 s free, whatever the body; the TTL sweeper may run late so an expired document is ignored); 05b after the TTL the same key and body is a new request. 06 every document has `expires_at` as an aware datetime within 24 h + 1 min of the clock, one at about 24 h. 07 a refused first attempt leaves the key free. 08 four racing retries over two instances: one record. 09 fault injection: the nth Firestore operation on a limiter document fails before it lands, or lands and fails (a lost answer), for every n: one record and one event per key in the end, a fixed 503 or the created answer, never a 500. 10 the same while replaying. 11 sixty replays leave the record collections untouched.

## 3. `store` (FR-MEM-09, SEC, PRD 16.5)
01 documents hold no uid, no raw key, no request or answer text (title, due text, display name, address). 02 documents are under `orgs/{org}/`, outside the people record collections. 03 a counter expires no sooner than the minute it counts and within a day. 04 no key, body or library error text in the logs or answers. 05 a limiter outage is the one fixed 503 `{"detail": "unavailable"}`, no-store, on six write routes, before anything is written; the call works again afterwards. 06 reads do not need the limiter store. 07 an outage never lets a write through and does not poison the key. 08 `erase_person` deletes that person's counter and keys, none of anyone else's, idempotently; their key and count are fresh afterwards. 09 a malformed id deletes nothing and raises. 10 an erase that failed can be run again to completion.

## 4. `event` (PE-12, PE-30, HR-1)
01 an append that landed and lost its answer is resent (same part id and content) and recorded once, for create, edit, done, snooze, not-mine and confirm. 02 an append that failed before landing is resent once and recorded once. 03 a store that stays down: the fixed 503, at most 3 attempts, one part id, the record undone, no echo of the error. 04 a replay of an idempotent create appends nothing. 05 property over scripts of `ok`/`lands`/`fail`: at most one event, one part id, at most 3 attempts; a 201 has exactly one event naming its record; a 503 left no record. 06 the same on Firestore stores. Guard: distinct requests never share a part id.

## 5. `emu` (skips without `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST`; never starts an emulator)
01 two clients share one count. 02 the 61st POST is 429 across two instances over HTTP. 03 a replay on another instance writes nothing; another body is 422. 04 three racing retries create one record. 05 the stored documents carry a real Timestamp expiry and no personal data. 06 erase deletes one person's documents. 07 no second client is built.

## 6. Defaults chosen (proposed BQ rows; the PRD names none)
- Key TTL 24 hours, counter TTL at least the minute and at most a day; an expired document that is still stored is ignored. The in-memory bounds of BQ-PPL11C-14 (16 keys per principal, 512 principals) do not apply to the durable store: the TTL and the 60 a minute cap bound it.
- A replay's answer holds no record text: the stored record may be a reference (record id, kind, body fingerprint) re-read on replay; an erased or undone record frees the key. Test: no text of a body or answer in any limiter document.
- The sliding minute stays a sliding minute (not a fixed window). Principals and keys are hashed in document ids and fields; the raw uid never is stored.
- Event writes make at most 3 `append` attempts with one part id; a part that landed on an ambiguous attempt and then never confirmed cannot be taken back (no compensating event type).
- The erase hook is a method on `Limits` (`erase_person`); registering a `tilo_memory.retention.RetentionStore` over it needs a decision, because the documents hold no uid for `erase_user`'s `record.user_id == user_id` match and no entity name has a window in `RETENTION_DAYS`.
