# Outbox rules beyond the schema (outbox.schema.json v1)

The outbox is the only path to a side effect (SEC-03): the harness writes an entry once the
deterministic policy engine has authorised it, and `tilo-connectors` runs it exactly once.
Rules marked "schema" are enforced by `outbox.schema.json`; the rest belong to the harness
validator (P0-09) and the connectors service.

| Rule | Rule | Checked by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OB-01 | An entry is written only after authorisation, and carries that authorisation unchanged. On a gate, `approval.requested` reserved the entry's `id` as its `outbox_id`, and the entry's `authz` equals the `authz` of the `approval.resolved` that answered `approve` on that gate (same `gate_id`, `card_id` and `outbox_id`): the person or Team Lead who tapped approve, who must be one who may open that gate (PRD 10.1): the world's owner (the actor of the world's `world.requested`), the Approver that `approval.requested` names in `approver` (its `user` equal to `authz.ref`), only when the world's owner made that assignment no later than `authz.at` (`approver.assigned_by`, the same owner-made stamp as a Team Lead's domain, so an Approver someone else named, a Team Lead naming themself included, or one assigned after the answer opens nothing), or a Team Lead when `approval.requested` names a `team_lead` allowance, the Team Lead's record (`team_lead.schema.json`, `id` equal to `authz.ref`) has that `domain` and was assigned by the world's owner no later than `authz.at` (`assigned_by`, PRD 16.2), and the policy the allowance names is the owner's own (its `owner` and every stamp, as below), has the gate's `action_type`, lists the domain in `team_lead_domains`, was created no later than `authz.at` and had not expired. A Viewer, anyone else, or a Team Lead of another domain opens nothing; the harness copies `approver` and `team_lead` from the owner's records, never from a model. An answer counts only from a signed-in session: `approval.resolved`'s `channel` is `web` or `mobile_web` (SEC-06 as amended by SD-01: no PIN; ADR-0019). One with `channel` `whatsapp`, a value the schema still allows, opens nothing, whatever the tier and whatever its `pin_verified`, which no rule reads. Without a gate, the entry's `authz` equals the `authz` of the `policy.applied` with this `outbox_id`, which is by `policy` with `ref` equal to its `policy_id` (EV-06; the check applies here too, so an entry and event both restamped as a person or Team Lead open nothing), and the policy named by `policy_id` covers the entry, since no person saw it: the policy has the `policy.applied`'s `action_type` and `undo_window_s`, was created (`created_by.at`) no later than `authz.at`, had not expired at `authz.at` and was at `policy.applied`'s `level` at `authz.at` (below); its `owner` is the world's owner (the actor of the world's `world.requested`), and only that owner made and changed it: `created_by.by`, `changed_by.by` (when set) and every `history` entry's `by` equal `owner`, since only an Owner creates and changes policies (PRD 10.1: a Team Lead only proposes, an Approver or Viewer has no say), so a record that anyone else created, changed or raised again covers nothing, whatever its `owner` says; `policy.applied`'s `connector` is in `scope.connectors` and its `district` in `scope.districts`; every one of the entry's `recipients` is in `scope.recipients` and at a domain in `scope.domains` (exact match); an empty scope list does not restrict. The level in force at `authz.at` is that of the policy's last `history` entry at or before `authz.at`, not the record's current level (history is oldest first and never empty, and the record's `level` equals its last entry's; a record that breaks either covers nothing). Revoking a policy and Stop all autopilot each append an entry at L1 (`policy.changed` schema), so a send after either is not covered, even if the owner later raised the level again, while a send made before a revocation stays covered (PRD 10.4 "revocable in one tap", FR-HIL-03, FR-HIL-07). At L2 ("instruction is approval", PRD 10.4, FR-HIL-05) the policy only lets the person's own instruction count as approval, given where Tilo knows who sent it: `policy.applied`'s `instruction_id` names a `world.requested` with `data.channel` `web` or `mobile_web` (a signed-in session) that the owner typed (ADR-0019). A `channel.message_in` is never an instruction, on any channel: an email's sender is not authenticated (email answers questions only and gates need sign-in, PRD 10.3, 11.1), WhatsApp is out of scope (SD-01), and a tapped option or any answer to a card is recorded by its own answering event, while only `approve` opens a gate (CR-07, BQ-56). A `world.requested` over `email` or `whatsapp` is never one either, whoever its `actor` is. The instruction is of the same world, earlier than `authz.at`, its `actor` is the policy's `owner` and its `data.destination` equals `policy.applied`'s `destination` (never null at L2), so a draft nobody asked for, a message from anyone else or an instruction about another thread opens nothing. Each instruction opens at most one entry: no two `policy.applied` of a world name the same `instruction_id`, whatever became of the first entry, so an entry cancelled in its undo window uses up its instruction and a redraft after Undo needs a new instruction or a gate. At L3 `instruction_id` is null. The engine opens it only while the policy's actions on the UTC day of `authz.at` (BQ-44), this one included, stay within `limits.per_day` and their credits, each `policy.applied`'s `credits`, within `limits.credits` (null does not restrict); otherwise it waits at a gate (FR-HIL-03, FR-HIL-06). A policy's limits span every world of its owner, not one world: every earlier `policy.applied` under the same `policy_id` that UTC day counts, in any of the owner's worlds, a cancelled one included, so whoever supplies the earlier events to the engine or validator (the harness, P0-09) passes the owner's `policy.applied` from all their worlds, or `per_day` would multiply by the number of worlds (BQ-52). A new recipient is T3 (OB-04), so no policy scope covers one. `approval.requested` never authorises (its `authz` is null, EV-05): a gate answered `edit` or `hold`, or not answered, writes nothing, and an entry that `approval.requested` reserved is opened only by `approval.resolved`. The `data.transfer` out that a sent entry emits carries the entry's `authz`, so a policy action is never shown as a person's approval or the reverse. Nothing waiting at a gate is in the outbox. | validator; policy engine (limits) | SEC-03, SEC-06, 10.1, 10.3, 10.4, 11.1, 16.2, FR-HIL-05, HR-7 |
| OB-02 | `scheduled_at` equals `created_at` plus `undo_window_s`. An entry waiting out its undo window is `scheduled`; with no window it is `queued`. | validator | 10.4 (L2), 11.4 (Undo) |
| OB-03 | `idempotency_key` is stable per step and unique per world; a retry reuses it and the connector runs a key at most once, so a retry never sends twice. | connectors | FR-AGT-11, FR-CHN-13 |
| OB-04 | T3 is authorised by a person or Team Lead, never a policy; any modifier makes the entry T3; an attachment is listed as the `attachments` modifier; below T3 there are no modifiers and no attachments. | schema | 10.4, 11.4 |
| OB-05 | The entry is exactly what was authorised. On a gate, `action_type`, `connector`, `recipients`, `attachments`, `item` and `destination` equal what the gate card showed (card rule CR-12). On either path, `action_type`, `risk_tier` and `item` (the same `artifact_id` and `version`) equal those of the event that reserved or opened it: `approval.requested` with this `outbox_id`, or `policy.applied` when a policy opened the gate. With no gate card on the policy path, `connector`, `destination` and `undo_window_s` also equal `policy.applied`'s, and at L2 that window is at least 1 s (`policy.applied` schema), so a policy send never loses its undo window, changes thread or connector, or changes tier. The policy engine assessed the tier modifiers, `safety_flag` included, on that version. `item` is null only when the action moves no content, such as a delete. A stored artifact version never changes, and the connector sends exactly `item` at its `version`, in `destination`. No field but `state` changes after the entry is written: a redraft (a new version) or any other change is a new proposal with a new gate, also during an undo window. States move `queued` or `scheduled` to `sending`, then `sent` or `failed`; `scheduled` may move to `cancelled` until sending starts. Each sent entry emits one `data.transfer` with direction `out`, this `outbox_id`, the same `connector` and `action_type`, and `artifact` equal to `item` (its `authz` is OB-01's). | validator | 10.3, 10.4, 11.4, HR-7 |

Fixtures: `fixtures/outbox/valid/` and `fixtures/outbox/invalid/` (one file per schema rule).
Cross-reference cases for OB-01 and OB-05 are in `fixtures/outbox/crossref/{valid,invalid}/`
(contracts-check skips that folder). Each file holds `rule` (null when valid), `note`, `entry`
(an outbox entry), three event parts, each `{type, authz, data}` as the event carries them or
null: `reserved_by` (the `approval.requested`, null when a policy opened the entry),
`authorised_by` (the `approval.resolved` or `policy.applied` that opened it, null when the gate
was never answered) and `transfer` (the `data.transfer` it emitted, null when not sent yet), and
`policy` (the `policy.schema.json` record `policy.applied` names or, on a gate a Team Lead
answered, the one `approval.requested`'s `team_lead` names; otherwise null), `team_lead` (the
`team_lead.schema.json` record of that Team Lead, null otherwise), `world_owner` (the user id of
the world's owner, on both paths), `instruction` (the
whole event `instruction_id` names, null on a gate path, at L3, or when no such event exists)
and `earlier_applied` (the `policy.applied` parts written earlier in any world of the policy's
owner, this world included, `[]` when none, null on a gate path; a part does not carry its
world, so a case's `note` says which came from another world).
Every part is shape-valid and passes EV-03 to EV-06; each invalid file breaks only the rule its
name starts with. The OB-01 cases cover a gate answered `hold`, a gate never answered, an
approval of another gate, `authz` from another person or from a Team Lead instead of the person,
a gate approved by a Viewer, by a Team Lead of another domain, by a Team Lead the policy does not
allow or whose domain someone other than the owner assigned, a gate approved over WhatsApp (with a PIN, ADR-0019), an Approver someone other than the owner
assigned, an Approver assigned after the answer, a Team Lead approving as a person through an
Approver slot they named themself,
a policy send stamped as a person or as a Team Lead, a person's approval stamped as a policy, a send stamped
differently from its entry, a policy send to a recipient, connector or district outside the
policy's scope, under an expired policy, a policy created after the send, a policy of someone
other than the world's owner, one someone else created under the owner's id, one someone else raised back to L2 after the owner revoked it, a policy revoked before the send, one that Stop all autopilot set
to L1 before the send and the owner raised again afterwards, a policy record whose `level` is
not its last history entry's, and an L2 send with no instruction, an instruction from someone
other than the policy's owner, one about another thread, one given after the send, one sent
by email (`channel.message_in` or `world.requested`), one an earlier, cancelled send already
used, the owner's typed WhatsApp answer to Tilo's question (a `channel.message_in`, ADR-0019), and an L3 send past its daily action limit or its daily credit limit (PI-11). The valid gate cases are approved by the owner, by the Approver the gate names (a T3 reply,
signed in on mobile web) and by an allowed Team Lead. The valid L2 cases take the instruction from
a signed-in request on the web or on mobile web; one was sent before its policy was revoked. A valid L3 case is the
last send its daily limits allow, with sends of the day before and under another policy that
do not count. A policy-path entry cannot be T3 or carry attachments: OB-04 rejects its shape
(`fixtures/outbox/invalid/t3_attachment_authorised_by_policy.json`), so it has no crossref case. The OB-05 policy cases
cover a dropped undo window, a different tier, a thread reply turned into a new email and a
send on another connector than the one assessed.
`tests/contracts/test_FR_WLD_03_outbox_crossref.py` holds the reference check; the P0-09
validator's tests must run the same files. Card-side cases (the entry against the gate card)
are CR-12's, in `fixtures/card/crossref/`.
