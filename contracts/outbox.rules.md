# Outbox rules beyond the schema (outbox.schema.json v1)

The outbox is the only path to a side effect (SEC-03): the harness writes an entry once the
deterministic policy engine has authorised it, and `tilo-connectors` runs it exactly once.
Rules marked "schema" are enforced by `outbox.schema.json`; the rest belong to the harness
validator (P0-09) and the connectors service.

| Rule | Rule | Checked by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OB-01 | An entry is written only after authorisation, and carries that authorisation unchanged. On a gate, `approval.requested` reserved the entry's `id` as its `outbox_id`, and the entry's `authz` equals the `authz` of the `approval.resolved` that answered `approve` on that gate (same `gate_id`, `card_id` and `outbox_id`): the person or Team Lead who tapped approve. Without a gate, the entry's `authz` equals the `authz` of the `policy.applied` with this `outbox_id` (by `policy`, `ref` equal to its `policy_id`, EV-06), and the policy named by `policy_id` covers the entry, since no person saw it: the policy has the `policy.applied`'s `action_type` and `undo_window_s`, was created (`created_by.at`) no later than `authz.at`, had not expired at `authz.at` and was at `policy.applied`'s `level` at `authz.at` (below); its `owner` is the world's owner (the actor of the world's `world.requested`; only an Owner creates policies, PRD 10.1); `policy.applied`'s `connector` is in `scope.connectors` and its `district` in `scope.districts`; every one of the entry's `recipients` is in `scope.recipients` and at a domain in `scope.domains` (exact match); an empty scope list does not restrict. The level in force at `authz.at` is that of the policy's last `history` entry at or before `authz.at`, not the record's current level (history is oldest first and never empty, and the record's `level` equals its last entry's; a record that breaks either covers nothing). Revoking a policy and Stop all autopilot each append an entry at L1 (`policy.changed` schema), so a send after either is not covered, even if the owner later raised the level again, while a send made before a revocation stays covered (PRD 10.4 "revocable in one tap", FR-HIL-03, FR-HIL-07). At L2 ("instruction is approval", PRD 10.4, FR-HIL-05) the policy only lets the person's own instruction count as approval, given where Tilo knows who sent it: `policy.applied`'s `instruction_id` names either a `world.requested` with `data.channel` `web` or `mobile_web` (a signed-in session) or a `channel.message_in` with `data.channel` `whatsapp` (the channels service sets its `actor` to the user whose number is bound and confirmed, after the webhook signature check; FR-CHN-02, FR-CHN-03, SEC-06). An email's sender is not authenticated, so a `channel.message_in` or `world.requested` over `email` is never an instruction, whoever its `actor` is (email answers questions only and gates need sign-in, PRD 10.3, 11.1). The instruction is of the same world, earlier than `authz.at`, its `actor` is the policy's `owner` and its `data.destination` equals `policy.applied`'s `destination` (never null at L2), so a draft nobody asked for, a message from anyone else or an instruction about another thread opens nothing. Each instruction opens at most one entry: no two `policy.applied` of a world name the same `instruction_id`, whatever became of the first entry, so an entry cancelled in its undo window uses up its instruction and a redraft after Undo needs a new instruction or a gate. At L3 `instruction_id` is null. The engine opens it only while the policy's actions that day, this one included, stay within `limits.per_day` and their credits within `limits.credits`; otherwise it waits at a gate (FR-HIL-03, FR-HIL-06). A new recipient is T3 (OB-04), so no policy scope covers one. `approval.requested` never authorises (its `authz` is null, EV-05): a gate answered `edit` or `hold`, or not answered, writes nothing, and an entry that `approval.requested` reserved is opened only by `approval.resolved`. The `data.transfer` out that a sent entry emits carries the entry's `authz`, so a policy action is never shown as a person's approval or the reverse. Nothing waiting at a gate is in the outbox. | validator; policy engine (limits) | SEC-03, SEC-06, 10.3, 10.4, 11.1, FR-HIL-05, HR-7 |
| OB-02 | `scheduled_at` equals `created_at` plus `undo_window_s`. An entry waiting out its undo window is `scheduled`; with no window it is `queued`. | validator | 10.4 (L2), 11.4 (Undo) |
| OB-03 | `idempotency_key` is stable per step and unique per world; a retry reuses it and the connector runs a key at most once, so a retry never sends twice. | connectors | FR-AGT-11, FR-CHN-13 |
| OB-04 | T3 is authorised by a person or Team Lead, never a policy; any modifier makes the entry T3; an attachment is listed as the `attachments` modifier; below T3 there are no modifiers and no attachments. | schema | 10.4, 11.4 |
| OB-05 | The entry is exactly what was authorised. On a gate, `action_type`, `connector`, `recipients`, `attachments`, `item` and `destination` equal what the gate card showed (card rule CR-12). On either path, `action_type`, `risk_tier` and `item` (the same `artifact_id` and `version`) equal those of the event that reserved or opened it: `approval.requested` with this `outbox_id`, or `policy.applied` when a policy opened the gate. With no gate card on the policy path, `connector`, `destination` and `undo_window_s` also equal `policy.applied`'s, and at L2 that window is at least 1 s (`policy.applied` schema), so a policy send never loses its undo window, changes thread or connector, or changes tier. The policy engine assessed the tier modifiers, `safety_flag` included, on that version. `item` is null only when the action moves no content, such as a delete. A stored artifact version never changes, and the connector sends exactly `item` at its `version`, in `destination`. No field but `state` changes after the entry is written: a redraft (a new version) or any other change is a new proposal with a new gate, also during an undo window. States move `queued` or `scheduled` to `sending`, then `sent` or `failed`; `scheduled` may move to `cancelled` until sending starts. Each sent entry emits one `data.transfer` with direction `out`, this `outbox_id`, the same `connector` and `action_type`, and `artifact` equal to `item` (its `authz` is OB-01's). | validator | 10.3, 10.4, 11.4, HR-7 |

Fixtures: `fixtures/outbox/valid/` and `fixtures/outbox/invalid/` (one file per schema rule).
Cross-reference cases for OB-01 and OB-05 are in `fixtures/outbox/crossref/{valid,invalid}/`
(contracts-check skips that folder). Each file holds `rule` (null when valid), `note`, `entry`
(an outbox entry), three event parts, each `{type, authz, data}` as the event carries them or
null: `reserved_by` (the `approval.requested`, null when a policy opened the entry),
`authorised_by` (the `approval.resolved` or `policy.applied` that opened it, null when the gate
was never answered) and `transfer` (the `data.transfer` it emitted, null when not sent yet), and
`policy` (the `policy.schema.json` record `policy.applied` names, null on a gate path),
`world_owner` (the user id of the world's owner, null on a gate path), `instruction` (the
whole event `instruction_id` names, null on a gate path, at L3, or when no such event exists)
and `earlier_applied` (the `policy.applied` parts written earlier in the same world, `[]` when
none, null on a gate path).
Every part is shape-valid and passes EV-03 to EV-06; each invalid file breaks only the rule its
name starts with. The OB-01 cases cover a gate answered `hold`, a gate never answered, an
approval of another gate, `authz` from another person or from a Team Lead instead of the person,
a policy send stamped as a person, a person's approval stamped as a policy, a send stamped
differently from its entry, a policy send to a recipient, connector or district outside the
policy's scope, under an expired policy, a policy created after the send, a policy of someone
other than the world's owner, a policy revoked before the send, one that Stop all autopilot set
to L1 before the send and the owner raised again afterwards, a policy record whose `level` is
not its last history entry's, and an L2 send with no instruction, an instruction from someone
other than the policy's owner, one about another thread, one given after the send, one sent
by email (`channel.message_in` or `world.requested`) and one an earlier, cancelled send already
used. The valid L2 cases take the instruction from the owner's WhatsApp answer and from a
signed-in web request; one was sent before its policy was revoked. The OB-05 policy cases
cover a dropped undo window, a different tier, a thread reply turned into a new email and a
send on another connector than the one assessed.
`tests/contracts/test_FR_WLD_03_outbox_crossref.py` holds the reference check; the P0-09
validator's tests must run the same files. Card-side cases (the entry against the gate card)
are CR-12's, in `fixtures/card/crossref/`.
