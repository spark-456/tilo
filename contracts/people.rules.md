# People, meetings, pinboards and calendar rules (CC-PEOPLE; person, meeting, minutes, work_item, pinboard_item, mention, calendar_entry, ui_action v1)

Source: `docs/prd-addendum-people-agents.md` (FR-MTG, FR-PA, FR-PB, FR-CAL), ADR-0014 (scope
extension to team scale) and ADR-0015 (these contracts and the privacy model). PRD 23.5 stays a
hard rule: Tilo never monitors individuals. The schemas check one record; the rules below need
other records, another field or the clock. `tilo-api` (CL-PEOPLE) checks them before it reads,
writes or serves a record, and its tests run the same `fixtures/person/crossref/` files as
`tests/contracts`. Validate against the JSON Schemas, never against the generated pydantic models
alone: generated models drop `if`/`then` (ADR-0002). Rule ids are stable. `PE-` is this file's
prefix (PRD 3.5 owns `EV-`, the PRD 3.1 principles own `PR-`).

Terms. A **member** of team T at time `now` is a person with a `memberships` entry for T whose
`assigned_by.by` is a person of the same org with `org_role: owner` and whose `assigned_by.at` is
no later than `now`. **Teammates** share at least one team at `now`. An **org owner** is a person
with `org_role: owner`; org roles are only ever promoted in this release, so a membership an
owner assigned stays valid. A **stamp** is a `created_by`, `set_up_by`, `assigned_by`,
`confirmed_by`, `attestation`, `removed` or history `by`/`at`. A **request** is
`{actor, action, view}`: `read_full` (every field), `read_status` (id, title, status, due),
`read_busy` (`calendar_entry.free_busy`), `read_directory` (id and display name),
`read_membership` (directory plus org role and memberships), `create`, `edit`, `confirm`
(proposed to active, open or accepted), `mention` (tag a person), `untag`, `mark_done`,
`assign_membership`, `set_org_role` and `start_capture`. A case may add the source `meeting` of a
Tilo-made record and, for a tag, the `mentions` the pin already has.

| Rule | Rule | Checked by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PE-01 | A personal pinboard item (`scope.kind: personal`) is read, listed, counted, searched, created, edited, removed and undone by `scope.user` only; Tilo creates one only under PE-08. No teammate, manager or org owner, and no assistant answer for another person, ever sees it; its events live in the owner's Desk world (PE-12). Its `owner` is null and it cannot be the subject of a mention. | validator (schema for `owner` and the mention team) | FR-PB-02, FR-PA-11; ADR-0014 |
| PE-02 | A team pinboard item that is `active` is read in full by the members of `scope.team` and by no one else. A `proposed` or `removed` one is read, confirmed or edited by its `set_up_by` person only. Creating, editing, confirming and removing a team item needs the actor to be a member of the team at the request (so a person who left keeps no edit right), and its `owner` (the responsible person) is null or a member at the write. A pin reaches a plate, digest or assistant answer of person X only while X may read it under this rule. | validator | FR-PB-01, FR-PB-05, FR-PB-07 |
| PE-03 | A `proposed` work item (only Tilo makes those, from minutes) is read by no one: the meeting Owner sees its content in the minutes, and only that Owner (`assigned_by.by`) confirms it. Once `open`, `done` or `dropped` the `owner` reads and edits it and marks it done; the person who assigned it reads its status only (`read_status`), never `source`, `evidence` or `history`; nobody else reads it. A person-made item is its creator's own (`owner` equals `created_by.ref`): nobody pushes an item onto another person's plate, a hand-over is a `reassign_requested` the owner accepts. | validator | FR-PA-01, FR-PA-11, FR-MTG-06 |
| PE-04 | A calendar entry is read in full by its `owner`. An `accepted` entry shared with a team (`share` names it, otherwise null; the schema allows `share` on `accepted` only) is also read in full by the members of that team, and the share lapses when the owner is no longer a member. Teammates of the owner get `read_busy` of `accepted` entries (interval only, no title, source or count) over a window of at most 31 days; a person who shares no team with the owner gets nothing, and a `proposed`, `declined` or `cancelled` entry shows to no one else. Org owners get no more than members. Sharing needs the owner to be a member of that team. | validator (schema for `share`) | FR-CAL-06 |
| PE-05 | A meeting, its transcript and its minutes are read in full by the meeting `owner` only. An attendee, an assignee, a teammate and an org owner read nothing of them; an assignee sees their own work item (PE-03) after confirmation and one evidence quote the API cuts from the transcript. Sharing minutes is an email, which is T3. | validator | FR-MTG-01, FR-MTG-10 |
| PE-06 | `mention`: only the `set_up_by` person of an `active` team item, who is a member, or Tilo for such an item whose `confirmed_by` is that person and dated no later than the request, tags a person, and the tagged person is of the pin's org and a member of the item's team at the request. A personal, `proposed` or `removed` item takes no mention. `untag`: the tagged person may always remove themselves; the item's `set_up_by` person may remove anyone; nobody else; a mention already removed is not removed again. A removed mention is never revived, and a person who removed themselves from a pin is not tagged on that pin again by anyone. | validator (schema for the team on a mention) | FR-PB-04 |
| PE-07 | `confirm` and `edit`. A Tilo-made team pin becomes `active`, a Tilo-made work item from a meeting becomes `open` and a Tilo-made calendar entry becomes `accepted` only with `confirmed_by`, and only by the person who owns the decision: the pin's `set_up_by`, the work item's `assigned_by.by` (the meeting Owner), the entry's `owner`. Tilo never confirms its own item. `confirmed_by.at` falls between the record's creation (`created_by.at`) and the request; a record whose confirmation names another person, or lies in the future, counts as not confirmed. An owner's `edit` of a work item never changes `status` or `confirmed_by`. | validator (schema for `confirmed_by`) | FR-MTG-06, FR-PB-06, FR-CAL-03 |
| PE-08 | Tilo's own entries carry `created_by.by: tilo` and a `source` the person sees as "added by Tilo from <source>" (schema). **Tilo writes a personal pin, a calendar proposal or a work item for person P only from a source P owns** (P's own meeting, mail or request); what comes from another person's meeting reaches P only after that meeting's Owner has confirmed the minutes (`meeting.minutes_confirmed_by` is set), as a work item or calendar proposal, never as a personal pin. A meeting source needs the meeting supplied, named by `source.ref` (before the `#`) and of the same org, or the write is refused; a request, mail or call source must belong to P. Tilo creates a calendar entry only as `proposed` with `confirmed_by` and `share` null (a Google sync creates the connected person's own entries, `share` null), never as `manual`, and never accepts, shares or confirms. A work item is Tilo-made from minutes only. On the personal board Tilo adds the pin `active` and the person can undo it (history `undone`, status `removed`). On the team board it starts `proposed`. | schema; validator | FR-PB-06, FR-MTG-06 |
| PE-09 | "Get in touch" is a drafted message to the `set_up_by` person through their own `preferred_channel`. It is a send to another person: a new recipient, T3, a card, `approval.resolved` by the sender (PIN on WhatsApp), the outbox, `authz` on `data.transfer`. The recipient is the `set_up_by` person's address or number resolved by `tilo-api`, never one a model or the entry text names. | outbox OB-01, OB-05 | FR-PB-03, 10.4 |
| PE-10 | The assistant panel (FR-PA-12 to -19) acts only through a `ui_action` that validates against `ui_action.schema.json`; the closed union, the route list and the target kinds in it are the allow-list. A model's output that does not validate is dropped and the answer says so. `mark_done` and `snooze` act on the signed-in person's own `open` work item; `tag` and `untag` follow PE-06; `draft_reply` makes a draft (T1) of a mail row on that person's plate, sends nothing and never takes the Autopilot path; a model-decided `mark_done`, `snooze`, `tag` or `untag` runs only after the person taps a confirm chip. A `navigate` target is a record the person may read under PE-01 to PE-05 and agrees with the route. No kind clicks, types, selects or sends. | validator; schema for the lists | FR-PA-14, FR-PA-16 |
| PE-11 | What the assistant reads for a person is what that person may read under PE-01 to PE-05 and PE-20, and nothing else. Every claim in `assistant.replied` cites `sources`; an `answered` outcome with no source is refused (schema). | validator; schema for `sources` | FR-PA-13, FR-PA-17, HR-1 |
| PE-12 | An event is written in a world whose owner may read its record in full under PE-01 to PE-05: events about a meeting, its minutes, and any proposed record whose content comes from another person's meeting go to the meeting Owner's meeting world or Desk world, never to an attendee's; events about a record P may read in full (P's own, or confirmed for P) go to P's Desk world. Those worlds have no Viewer, Approver or Team Lead scope. This covers `meeting.started` and `meeting.ended` that carry a `meeting_id`. Until ADR-0009 (owner-only `vis`) lands, `tilo-api` refuses to share such a world; afterwards these events carry `vis: []` wherever `district` is null. Team boards are read from their records, never from another person's event stream. The `summary` of these events names no title, quote, address or link, only display names and counts; their payloads carry no artifact label. | validator | SEC-07, FR-HIL-09 |
| PE-13 | What a synced Google event keeps is the title, the start and end, whether it is all day and a count of other attendees. No description, no attendee names or addresses, no conference link and no location is stored or sent anywhere (schema: no such field). Disconnecting Calendar, or deleting the person, deletes the person's cached entries (FR-MEM-09). A pinboard `url` is https to a named host with no user info, query, fragment or path parameter, passes the secret redactor before it is stored (a bare token can look like a path), is display-only and is fetched by no Tilo service. Meeting titles, agenda titles and attendee names hold no link. | schema; validator | FR-CAL-02, FR-CAL-08, 16.5 |
| PE-14 | A calendar write to Google is an outbox entry. An event on the person's own calendar with `attendee_count: 0` is T1 with the undo window; any entry with `attendee_count > 0` is T3 (`new_recipients`), always a person. | outbox OB-01; `config/autonomy.yaml` | FR-CAL-04 |
| PE-15 | Minutes: a `verified` decision or action item cites at least one utterance (schema). A resolved `owner` has `owner_text`; two or more people matching the same spoken name leave `owner` null and raise one question card (validator). `due` is a date computed by code from `due_text`, the meeting date and the assignee's `time_zone`, never by a model. The schema allows 60 extracted action items; at most 20 per meeting are offered for confirmation, the rest are listed unconfirmed. | schema; validator | FR-MTG-04, FR-MTG-05, FR-MTG-06 |
| PE-16 | A person's data is exported and deleted with them: persons, work items, personal pins, calendar entries, meetings they own, their Desk world with its events, and the archived assistant messages (`assistant.asked` and `assistant.replied` `message_ref`, kept 30 days like any raw-archive record). Team items they set up stay, set up by "a former member". Transcripts are deleted at `delete_transcript_after`, minutes at `delete_minutes_after`. | validator; FR-MEM-09 script | 16.5, FR-MTG-10 |
| PE-17 | Directory changes are an org owner's: a `person` record is created, its `org_role` set and a `memberships` entry assigned only by a person of the same org whose `org_role` is `owner`. The first person of an org is minted by `tilo-api` as its owner (the org id never comes from sign-up input); every other `created_by` and `assigned_by` names an owner. A member cannot make themselves an owner or add themselves to a team; a stamp that does not name such an owner grants nothing (PE-02). | validator | FR-PB-01, ADR-0014 |
| PE-18 | Capture starts only for a meeting whose `attestation.by` is its `owner`, a person of the org (the consent gate for who may record, FR-MTG-01); the request is made by that owner. | validator | FR-MTG-01, 16.5 |
| PE-19 | **Stamps and couplings.** `tilo-api` writes every stamp from the authenticated actor and the server clock, never from a request body, a model output, an import or sign-up input. On every read, confirm, edit and tag, not only on create, it also checks (and fails closed when a meeting-sourced record arrives without its meeting): a Tilo-made pin's `set_up_by` and a Tilo-made work item's `assigned_by.by` are the owner of the meeting `source.ref` names; `meeting.minutes_confirmed_by` is by the meeting owner, dated no later than the request, and set only on a `minuted` meeting (schema); a person-made work item's `assigned_by` is null or its creator; a person-made pin's `set_up_by` equals `created_by.ref`; `confirmed`/`accepted` history entries are by `confirmed_by.by`; a `link` pin has a `url` and other kinds none; a calendar entry or busy interval ends no earlier than it starts; a `free_busy` window is at most 31 days; `snooze.until` is within 30 days of the request; a `navigate` route and target kind agree. | validator | all |
| PE-20 | **Person records.** A person reads their own record in full. Teammates read the directory view only (id, display name). Org owners also read `org_role` and `memberships`. Email, aliases, time zone and preferred channel never leave `tilo-api` except to the person (a recipient is resolved server side, PE-09). A person outside the team reads nothing, not even a display name, except through a team they share. | validator | FR-PB-03, FR-PB-04, 16.5 |
| PE-21 | **Flagged content.** A work item, pin or calendar entry made from minutes, mail or calendar text the safety provider flagged has `flagged: true` (schema); a model never clears it. Any draft, nudge, send, invite or calendar write built from a flagged record is T3 with `safety_flag`: a person decides, with PIN on WhatsApp, and Autopilot never sends it. | schema (field); outbox and policy engine (tier) | 16.3, SEC-04, FR-PA-09 |

## Fixtures

- `fixtures/<name>/valid|invalid/` for `person`, `meeting`, `minutes`, `work_item`,
  `pinboard_item`, `mention`, `calendar_entry`, `ui_action`; `fixtures/calendar_entry.agenda_item/`
  and `fixtures/calendar_entry.free_busy/` exercise those `$defs`. Invalid file names say the
  rule they break.
- `fixtures/person/crossref/{valid,invalid}/` (contracts-check skips this folder): each file is
  `{rule, note, now, persons, record, request}`. `persons` maps ids to `person` records, `record`
  is `{kind, data}` (a `pinboard_item`, `work_item`, `calendar_entry`, `meeting`, `person` or
  `mention`; a mention also holds the `pinboard_item` it tags in `pin` and may hold the `mentions`
  the pin already has; a Tilo-made record may hold its source `meeting`), and `request` is
  `{actor, action, view}`. Every part is shape-valid. A valid file is a request the rules grant
  (`rule` is null); each invalid file is a request they refuse and breaks only the rule its `rule`
  field and name state. The `tilo-api` tests run the same files. Rules a schema enforces (a
  `share` on a proposed entry, a Meet link in a title) have an invalid schema fixture instead.
- `fixtures/events/valid/<type>.json` for each new event type (registry, below).

## Events (registry 1.1.0)

`meeting.minuted`, `work_item.created`, `work_item.changed`, `pinboard.item_added`,
`pinboard.item_changed`, `mention.added`, `mention.removed`, `calendar.synced`,
`calendar.entry_proposed`, `calendar.entry_changed`, `assistant.asked`, `assistant.replied`:
none is a side effect and none carries `authz`; a send, an invite or a calendar write is an
outbox entry with its own `data.transfer`. Payloads carry ids, counts and enums, never a title,
a transcript line, a message body, an address, a link or an artifact label: an assistant message
points at the redacted raw-archive record (`message_ref`). Each is written in the world PE-12
names. `meeting.started` and `meeting.ended` gain optional `meeting_id` and, on `meeting.ended`,
`reason` (1.1.0, additive).
