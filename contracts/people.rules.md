# People, meetings, pinboards and calendar rules (CC-PEOPLE; person, meeting, minutes, work_item, pinboard_item, mention, calendar_entry, ui_action, transcript, plate v1)

Source: `docs/prd-addendum-people-agents.md` (FR-MTG, FR-PA, FR-PB, FR-CAL), ADR-0015 (scope
extension to team scale), ADR-0016 (these contracts and the privacy model), ADR-0020 (evidence
quotes, PE-22 to PE-25), ADR-0021 (the plate, PE-26 to PE-30) and ADR-0040 (team-shared worlds, PE-31 to PE-35). PRD 23.5
stays a hard rule: Tilo never monitors individuals. The schemas check one record; the rules below need
other records, another field or the clock. `tilo-api` (CL-PEOPLE) checks them before it reads,
writes or serves a record, and its tests run the same `fixtures/person/crossref/` files as
`tests/contracts`. Validate against the JSON Schemas, never against the generated pydantic models
alone: generated models drop `if`/`then` (ADR-0002). Rule ids are stable. `PE-` is this file's
prefix (PRD 3.5 owns `EV-`, the PRD 3.1 principles own `PR-`).

Terms. A **member** of team T at time `now` is a person with a `memberships` entry for T whose
`assigned_by.by` is a person of the same org with `org_role: owner` and whose `assigned_by.at` is
no later than `now`. **Teammates** share at least one team at `now`. An **org owner** is a person
with `org_role: owner`; org roles are only ever promoted in this release, so a membership an
owner assigned stays valid. A **stamp** is a `created_by`, `set_up_by`, `assigned_by`,
`confirmed_by`, `attestation`, `removed`, a world share's `granted_by` or `revoked`, or history `by`/`at`.
A **request** is `{actor, action, view}`: `read_full` (every field), `read_status` (id, title, status, due),
`read_busy` (`calendar_entry.free_busy`), `read_directory` (id and display name),
`read_membership` (directory plus org role and memberships), `read_team` (the team view of a work item in a
shared world: id, title, status, due, owner, world_id, PE-33), `create`, `edit`, `confirm`
(proposed to active, open or accepted), `mention` (tag a person), `untag`, `mark_done`,
`assign_membership`, `set_org_role` and `start_capture`. A case may add the source `meeting` of a
Tilo-made record and, for a tag, the `mentions` the pin already has.

| PE-01 | A personal pinboard item (`scope.kind: personal`) is read, listed, counted, searched, created, edited, removed and undone by `scope.user` only; Tilo creates one only under PE-08. No teammate, manager or org owner, and no assistant answer for another person, ever sees it; its events live in the owner's Desk world (PE-12). Its `owner` is null and it cannot be the subject of a mention. | validator (schema for `owner` and the mention team) | FR-PB-02, FR-PA-11; ADR-0015 |
| PE-02 | A team pinboard item that is `active` is read in full by the members of `scope.team` and by no one else. A `proposed` or `removed` one is read, confirmed or edited by its `set_up_by` person only. Creating, editing, confirming and removing a team item needs the actor to be a member of the team at the request (so a person who left keeps no edit right), and its `owner` (the responsible person) is null or a member at the write. A pin reaches a plate, digest or assistant answer of person X only while X may read it under this rule. | validator | FR-PB-01, FR-PB-05, FR-PB-07 |
| PE-03 | A `proposed` work item (only Tilo makes those, from minutes) is read by no one: the meeting Owner sees its content in the minutes, and only that Owner (`assigned_by.by`) confirms it. Once `open`, `done` or `dropped` the `owner` reads and edits it and marks it done; the person who assigned it reads its status only (`read_status`), never `source`, `evidence` or `history`; nobody else reads it, with one exception (PE-33, ADR-0040): a person-made item that names a `world_id` is also read as the team view (`read_team`: id, title, status, due, owner, world) by the members of a team that world is shared with (PE-31), while it is `open` or `done` and not `flagged`. A person-made item is its creator's own (`owner` equals `created_by.ref`): nobody pushes an item onto another person's plate, a hand-over is a `reassign_requested` the owner accepts. | validator (schema for `world_id`) | FR-PA-01, FR-PA-11, FR-MTG-06; ADR-0040 |
| PE-04 | A calendar entry is read in full by its `owner`. An `accepted` entry shared with a team (`share` names it, otherwise null; the schema allows `share` on `accepted` only) is also read in full by the members of that team, and the share lapses when the owner is no longer a member. Teammates of the owner get `read_busy` of `accepted` entries (interval only, no title, source or count) over a window of at most 31 days; a person who shares no team with the owner gets nothing, and a `proposed`, `declined` or `cancelled` entry shows to no one else. Org owners get no more than members. Sharing needs the owner to be a member of that team. | validator (schema for `share`) | FR-CAL-06 |
| PE-05 | A meeting, its transcript and its minutes are read in full by the meeting `owner` only. An attendee, an assignee, a teammate and an org owner read nothing of them; an assignee sees their own work item (PE-03) after confirmation and one evidence quote the API cuts from the transcript. Sharing minutes is an email, which is T3. | validator | FR-MTG-01, FR-MTG-10 |
| PE-06 | `mention`: only the `set_up_by` person of an `active` team item, who is a member, or Tilo for such an item whose `confirmed_by` is that person and dated no later than the request, tags a person, and the tagged person is of the pin's org and a member of the item's team at the request. A personal, `proposed` or `removed` item takes no mention. `untag`: the tagged person may always remove themselves; the item's `set_up_by` person may remove anyone; nobody else; a mention already removed is not removed again. A removed mention is never revived, and a person who removed themselves from a pin is not tagged on that pin again by anyone. | validator (schema for the team on a mention) | FR-PB-04 |
| PE-07 | `confirm` and `edit`. A Tilo-made team pin becomes `active`, a Tilo-made work item from a meeting becomes `open` and a Tilo-made calendar entry becomes `accepted` only with `confirmed_by`, and only by the person who owns the decision: the pin's `set_up_by`, the work item's `assigned_by.by` (the meeting Owner), the entry's `owner`. Tilo never confirms its own item. `confirmed_by.at` falls between the record's creation (`created_by.at`) and the request; a record whose confirmation names another person, or lies in the future, counts as not confirmed. An owner's `edit` of a work item changes only its title, due date and snooze; `status` moves only by the named actions (`mark_done`, `confirm`), and `source`, `owner`, `assigned_by`, `created_by` and `confirmed_by` never change after creation. | validator (schema for `confirmed_by`) | FR-MTG-06, FR-PB-06, FR-CAL-03 |
| PE-08 | Tilo's own entries carry `created_by.by: tilo` and a `source` the person sees as "added by Tilo from <source>" (schema). **Tilo writes a personal pin, a calendar proposal or a work item for person P only from a source P owns** (P's own meeting, mail or request); what comes from another person's meeting reaches P only as one action item the meeting Owner released: a work item the Owner confirmed (`confirmed_by` by `assigned_by.by`) on minutes the Owner confirmed (`meeting.minutes_confirmed_by`), and a calendar proposal that cites that item (`source.ref` `mt_x#aiN`, with the work item supplied), never a bare transcript or meeting reference, an item the Owner rejected, or a personal pin. A meeting source needs the meeting supplied, named by `source.ref` (before the `#`) and of the same org, or the write is refused; a request, mail or call source must belong to P, and a Google sync writes the connected person's own entries only (`source_owner` is the owner). Tilo creates a calendar entry only as `proposed` with `confirmed_by` and `share` null (a Google sync creates the connected person's own entries, `share` null), never as `manual`, and never accepts, shares or confirms. A work item is Tilo-made from minutes only. On the personal board Tilo adds the pin `active` and the person can undo it (history `undone`, status `removed`). On the team board it starts `proposed`. | schema; validator | FR-PB-06, FR-MTG-06 |
| PE-09 | "Get in touch" is a drafted message to the `set_up_by` person through their own `preferred_channel` (in the app or by email, FR-PB-03). It is a send to another person: a new recipient, T3, a card, `approval.resolved` by the sender in a signed-in web or mobile-web session (SEC-06 as amended; an email link never approves), the outbox, `authz` on `data.transfer`. The recipient is the `set_up_by` person's address resolved by `tilo-api`, never one a model or the entry text names. | outbox OB-01, OB-05 | FR-PB-03, 10.4 |
| PE-10 | The assistant panel (FR-PA-12 to -19) acts only through a `ui_action` that validates against `ui_action.schema.json`; the closed union, the route list and the target kinds in it are the allow-list. A model's output that does not validate is dropped and the answer says so. `mark_done` and `snooze` act on the signed-in person's own `open` work item; `tag` and `untag` follow PE-06; `draft_reply` makes a draft (T1) of a mail row on that person's plate, sends nothing and never takes the Autopilot path; a model-decided `mark_done`, `snooze`, `tag` or `untag` runs only after the person taps a confirm chip. A `navigate` target is a record the person may read under PE-01 to PE-05 and agrees with the route. No kind clicks, types, selects or sends. | validator; schema for the lists | FR-PA-14, FR-PA-16 |
| PE-11 | What the assistant reads for a person is what that person may read under PE-01 to PE-05 and PE-20, and nothing else. Every claim in `assistant.replied` cites `sources`; an `answered` outcome with no source is refused (schema). | validator; schema for `sources` | FR-PA-13, FR-PA-17, HR-1 |
| PE-12 | An event is written in a world whose owner may read its record in full under PE-01 to PE-05: events about a meeting, its minutes, and any proposed record whose content comes from another person's meeting go to the meeting Owner's meeting world or Desk world, never to an attendee's; events about a record P may read in full (P's own, or confirmed for P) go to P's Desk world. Those worlds have no Viewer, Approver or Team Lead scope. This covers `meeting.started` and `meeting.ended` that carry a `meeting_id`. Until ADR-0009 (owner-only `vis`) lands, `tilo-api` refuses to share such a world; afterwards these events carry `vis: []` wherever `district` is null. Team boards, and the team view of a shared world's work items (PE-33), are read from their records, never from another person's event stream. The `summary` of these events names no title, quote, address or link, only display names and counts; their payloads carry no artifact label. | validator | SEC-07, FR-HIL-09 |
| PE-13 | What a synced Google event keeps is the title, the start and end, whether it is all day and a count of other attendees. No description, no attendee names or addresses, no conference link and no location is stored or sent anywhere (schema: no such field). The one exception is the join link of a meeting Tilo hosts: read from the host account's own event when its owner clicks Join, sent to that owner's browser only and stored nowhere (MJ-01 to MJ-10, `meeting_join.rules.md`, ADR-0024 amendment 4); the synced copy still keeps no link. Disconnecting Calendar, or deleting the person, deletes the person's cached entries (FR-MEM-09). A pinboard `url` is https to a named host with no user info, query, fragment or path parameter, passes the secret redactor before it is stored (a bare token can look like a path), is display-only and is fetched by no Tilo service. Meeting titles, agenda titles and attendee names hold no link and no character that could hide one (schema, `meeting` 1.2.0, ADR-0016 decision 14): no `://` or backslash form of it; no meeting host meet.google.com, teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, zoom.us, webex.com or whereby.com, alone or as a suffix, in any case; no bare `host.tld/segment` (g.co/meet included; a last label `js` is not a host, so `Node.js/React` stays); no Cc, Zl, Zp, Basic Multilingual Plane Cf, Hangul filler or U+2800; an invisible combining mark (U+034F, variation selectors and the like) only after a non-ASCII character or, for U+FE0E and U+FE0F, a keycap base. Astral format and default-ignorable characters (U+E0000 to U+E0FFF and the other astral Cf) cannot be named portably in a pattern, so the validator handles them: `tilo-api` removes astral Cf with `tilo_people.text.clean_line` and removes or refuses the other astral default-ignorables before the schema check (BQ-MLINK-3). | schema; validator | FR-CAL-02, FR-CAL-08, FR-MTG-01, 16.5 |
| PE-14 | A calendar write to Google is an outbox entry. An event on the person's own calendar with `attendee_count: 0` is T1 with the undo window; any entry with `attendee_count > 0` is T3 (`new_recipients`), always a person. | outbox OB-01; `config/autonomy.yaml` | FR-CAL-04 |
| PE-15 | Minutes: a `verified` decision or action item cites at least one utterance (schema). A resolved `owner` has `owner_text`; two or more people matching the same spoken name leave `owner` null and raise one question card (validator). `due` is a date computed by code from `due_text`, the meeting date and the assignee's `time_zone`, never by a model. The schema allows 60 extracted action items; at most 20 per meeting are offered for confirmation, the rest are listed unconfirmed. | schema; validator | FR-MTG-04, FR-MTG-05, FR-MTG-06 |
| PE-16 | A person's data is exported and deleted with them: persons, work items, personal pins, calendar entries, meetings they own, their Desk world with its events, and the archived assistant messages (`assistant.asked` and `assistant.replied` `message_ref`, kept 30 days like any raw-archive record). Team items they set up stay, set up by "a former member". Transcripts are deleted at `delete_transcript_after`, minutes at `delete_minutes_after`. | validator; FR-MEM-09 script | 16.5, FR-MTG-10 |
| PE-17 | Directory changes are an org owner's: a `person` record is created, its `org_role` set and a `memberships` entry assigned only by a person of the same org whose `org_role` is `owner`. The first person of an org is minted by `tilo-api` as its owner (the org id never comes from sign-up input); every other `created_by` and `assigned_by` names an owner. A member cannot make themselves an owner or add themselves to a team; a stamp that does not name such an owner grants nothing (PE-02). | validator | FR-PB-01, ADR-0015 |
| PE-18 | Capture starts only for a meeting whose `attestation.by` is its `owner`, a person of the org (the consent gate for who may record, FR-MTG-01); the request is made by that owner. | validator | FR-MTG-01, 16.5 |
| PE-19 | **Stamps and couplings.** `tilo-api` writes every stamp from the authenticated actor and the server clock, never from a request body, a model output, an import or sign-up input. On every read, confirm, edit and tag, not only on create, it also checks (and fails closed when a meeting-sourced record arrives without its meeting): a Tilo-made pin's `set_up_by` and a Tilo-made work item's `assigned_by.by` are the owner of the meeting `source.ref` names; `meeting.minutes_confirmed_by` is by the meeting owner, dated no later than the request, and set only on a `minuted` meeting (schema); a person-made work item's `assigned_by` is null or its creator; a person-made pin's `set_up_by` equals `created_by.ref`; `confirmed`/`accepted` history entries are by `confirmed_by.by`; a `link` pin has a `url` and other kinds none; a calendar entry or busy interval ends no earlier than it starts; a `free_busy` window is at most 31 days; `snooze.until` is within 30 days of the request; deleting a meeting leaves a tombstone (id, org, owner, `minutes_confirmed_by`) so these checks still hold for the records made from it; a `navigate` route and target kind agree. | validator | all |
| PE-20 | **Person records.** A person reads their own record in full. Teammates read the directory view only (id, display name). Org owners also read `org_role` and `memberships`. Email, aliases, time zone and preferred channel never leave `tilo-api` except to the person (a recipient is resolved server side, PE-09). A person outside the team reads nothing, not even a display name, except through a team they share. | validator | FR-PB-03, FR-PB-04, 16.5 |
| PE-21 | **Flagged content.** A work item, pin or calendar entry made from minutes, mail or calendar text the safety provider flagged has `flagged: true` (schema); a model never clears it. Any draft, nudge, send, invite or calendar write built from a flagged record is T3 with `safety_flag`: a person decides in a signed-in web or mobile-web session (SEC-06 as amended), and Autopilot never sends it. | schema (field); outbox and policy engine (tier) | 16.3, SEC-04, FR-PA-09 |
| PE-22 | **Evidence quotes are computed, never kept.** `tilo-api` cuts a quote per request from the record that cites the utterance, the meeting record and the transcript version the minutes pin, read through the artifact store with the meeting's own scope (org, `world_id`, owner, all from the stored meeting, never from the request; BQ-273). Nothing new is stored: a quote never enters an event, a log, the raw archive, an email or digest, a notification, a cache that outlives the request or a model prompt (`record_ref` has no transcript kind). It expires with the transcript (ADR-0020 decision 2): after `delete_transcript_after` or a sweep, or once the meeting is missing, discarded or deleted, a reader PE-23 admits gets `expired` with `transcript` null; the PE-19 tombstone keeps the owner and needs no transcript reference (BQ-CCP3-2). Ingest (FR-MTG-02) writes a `Transcript` whose utterance ids strictly increase. | validator; schema (`record_ref`, closed event payloads) | ADR-0020; FR-MTG-02, FR-MTG-10 |
| PE-23 | **Who gets a quote.** Owner route (`GET /v1/meetings/{id}/quotes`, 1 to 20 utterance ids): only the meeting owner, from the stored record or its PE-19 tombstone, gets an answer: one quote per id while the meeting is live (S-26 asks for the ids a minutes item cites), every id `expired` with `transcript` null once it is discarded, deleted or past `delete_transcript_after`. Everyone else gets a 404 whatever the meeting's state (ADR-0020 decision 3), and a meeting with neither record nor tombstone answers a 404 to everyone, since nobody can be its owner (BQ-CCP3-2). Item route (`GET /v1/work-items/{id}/quote`): a work item's owner who is not the meeting owner gets at most one, only while PE-03 lets them read the Tilo-made item the meeting owner confirmed (`open`, `done`, `dropped`), on minutes the owner confirmed (PE-08), for an utterance cited by both `source.evidence` and the minutes action item `source.ref` names (whose `work_item_id` is the item): the lowest such id present in the pinned version, else the lowest cited (BQ-265); no meeting title (BQ-266); none when the two share no id. When the item's meeting is missing, discarded or deleted, or its transcript is swept, the item's owner gets `expired` for the lowest utterance the item itself cites, the same id on every path. Anyone who may not read the item (the assigner, attendees, teammates, org owners, another org) gets the item's own 404 (PE-03). A live meeting without `world_id` or not `minuted` answers a 404 to its owner on the owner route and to the item's owner on the item route. | validator (crossref) | ADR-0020; PE-05 |
| PE-24 | **Redaction and untrusted text.** For a viewer other than the meeting owner a quote is `withheld` unless the transcript version is `screening: clean` and the minutes are not `flagged` (BQ-267). A shown quote has email addresses, phone numbers and links replaced by `[removed]` and passes the secret redactor (SEC-01); its speaker is the display name (PE-20) of the attendee the label resolved to (PE-15) when that person is the viewer or a teammate, else null, shown as "A participant" (BQ-268). For every viewer code removes control, format and line or paragraph separator characters (Unicode Cc, Cf, Zl, Zp) from the text and from the speaker, label or display name alike, and cuts the text at 240 characters on a word boundary (`truncated`). The schema (`quote_line`) refuses Cc, Zl, Zp and every Basic Multilingual Plane Cf; astral Cf (the tag characters U+E0000 to U+E007F and others) is the validator's alone; the web renders plain text, with no Markdown, HTML or link detection. | schema (`quote_line`); validator | ADR-0020; SEC-01 |
| PE-25 | **Honest quote states (HR-1).** `shown` only when the utterance is in the pinned version; `missing` when it is not (an FR-MTG-04 fault, logged by id, never shown as backed); `expired` (PE-22); `withheld` (PE-24). Only `shown` carries `text`; the others have `text` and `speaker` null and `truncated` false, and `expired` also has `transcript` null (schema) and goes only to a reader PE-23 admits. | schema; validator | ADR-0020; HR-1 |
| PE-26 | **One plate builder, never stored.** `tilo-api` builds the plate per request for the signed-in principal only (`user`); no route, query or argument names another person, so there is no manager view. It is never stored and never an event. S-27, the digest (FR-PA-06) and the panel (FR-PA-13) read this builder's `rows`, so they show the same ids. | validator (crossref) | ADR-0021; FR-PA-02 |
| PE-27 | **What joins the plate.** `rows`: the person's own `open` work items, not snoozed at `as_of` and with a last change other than `reassign_requested` (never `proposed`, PE-03); unanswered, unexpired cards in worlds they own or gates naming them as Approver (ADR-0006); runs of worlds they own that are running or blocked; mail the triage store holds as `needs_reply` for their own connected account and not yet answered or dismissed; active team pins they own or carry an active mention of them while PE-02 lets them read the pin, and their own active personal pins with a `due` (BQ-272). Nothing from a world where they are only a Viewer or a Team Lead. `parked`: their own work items snoozed until `snoozed_until`, asked to be reassigned until the assigner acts, or done in the last 24 hours (BQ-271); the digest and the panel ignore it. At most 200 rows and 100 parked. `truncated` is true only when more rows that pass these rules exist than the cap, counted after the PE-26 and PE-27 filters, so it says nothing about records the person may not read; the schema then requires a full lane (200 rows or 100 parked). | validator (crossref) | ADR-0021; BQ-269 to BQ-272 |
| PE-28 | **Groups and order are code (PR-9).** `attention`: gate, consent and blocked cards and blocked runs (schema); `overdue`: `due` before `today`; `soon`: `due` from `today` to `today` + 2 days, today's calendar entries, question and meeting cards; `rest`: everything else. `today` is the date in the person's `time_zone` at `as_of`. Within a group: `due` ascending, then `at` ascending (null last for both), then `ref.kind`, then `ref.id`. The `parked` lanes run `snoozed`, `asked`, `done`, ordered the same way inside. | validator; schema for `attention` | ADR-0021; FR-PA-02 |
| PE-29 | **What a row carries.** A reference and at most a title: the record's own (work item, pin or calendar entry title, card header, the run brief's goal cut to 140 characters), cleaned like a quote line (PE-24), null for mail (BQ-269). `due` is the item's, pin's or brief's date; `at` the card's creation, the run's start, the item's creation, the mail's receipt, the pin's last change or the entry's start; `from` an assigner other than the person or a pin's setter, named only under PE-20, and null on mail (schema). No quote, body, address, link or transcript line (schema: closed row). `ref.kind` and `reason` agree (a card: gate, consent, blocked, question or meeting_card; a world: blocked or run; each other kind its one reason). `flagged` copies the record's (PE-21); `evidence` is true only for a meeting-sourced work item that cites an utterance (BQ-CCP3-1). `actions` is a hint from PE-03, PE-07 and PE-10 (`not_mine` only on an item someone else assigned, `draft_reply` only on mail), checked again at the write. | schema; validator | ADR-0021 |
| PE-30 | **Honest changes (HR-1).** A row moves or leaves only after its write commits and its event (`work_item.changed`, `approval.resolved`, ...) is recorded; no optimistic move. Plate actions write the existing `work_item.changed` (`done`, `snoozed`, `reassign_requested`, `reopened`) in the person's Desk world (PE-12); there is no plate event. `fingerprint` is the SHA-256 of `rows` as JSON with sorted keys, no whitespace, UTF-8. It leaves out `user`, so the panel's cache key (FR-PA-19) is (`user`, `fingerprint`, question), never the fingerprint alone. | validator; web | ADR-0021; FR-PA-19 |
| PE-31 | **World share.** A `world_share` links one world to one team. It is made only by the world's owner (the record owner from the store, never the request) in a full web or mobile-web sign-in session, for a team the owner is a member of at the request; `granted_by` is that owner and, like `revoked`, is stamped from the session and the server clock (PE-19). Only that owner revokes, and it takes effect on the next request. The `id` is `ws_` and the first 32 hex characters of the SHA-256 of `<world_id>/<team>`. A share is **in force** at `now` only while `revoked` is null, `granted_by.by` is the world's current owner, `granted_by.at` is no later than `now`, `org_id` is the owner's org, and the owner is a member of the team (PE-02 terms) with a membership assigned no later than `granted_by.at`, so it lapses when the owner leaves the team and does not revive when they return. Any other state is no share, never an error. A share is not a role: Approvers and Team Leads come only from their own assignments (ADR-0006). | validator (schema for the shape) | ADR-0040; PRD 10.1 |
| PE-32 | **Team reader.** A person who does not own a world reads it only as a team reader: a full web or mobile-web sign-in session of the same org, bound to no world (never an email, signed-link, demo or world-bound session), who is a member of the team of a share in force for that world (PE-31) at the request. They get the Viewer view (PRD 10.1) of every district: events whose `vis` is `all` or lists a district, the layout, the delivery, and the artifacts and runs those events name; never an event with `vis: []` (PE-12), a card, the console, costs, a policy, a kill switch, a schedule or any write. The check runs on every request from the verified session, the world record and the directory, never from a header, query, path team or body, so a revoked, lapsed or removed reader loses access on the next request, and every refusal (an unknown world and a world not shared with the person alike) is the one unknown-world 404. | API tests | ADR-0040; SEC-07, FR-HIL-09 |
| PE-33 | **Work items in a shared world.** `world_id` is set only when a person creates an item they own and made (`created_by.by` person, `owner` equals `created_by.ref`), who owns that world or is a team reader of it (PE-32) at the create; it never changes, and Tilo never sets one (a meeting's items stay personal, PE-08). `read_team` of an item is allowed to a person of the item's org who is a member of the team of a share in force (PE-31) for its `world_id` when the item's `owner` is also a member of that team at the request, its `status` is `open` or `done` and `flagged` is false. ## Events (registry 1.1.0; `world.share_changed` since 1.5.0)

`meeting.minuted`, `work_item.created`, `work_item.changed`, `pinboard.item_added`,
`pinboard.item_changed`, `mention.added`, `mention.removed`, `calendar.synced`,
`calendar.entry_proposed`, `calendar.entry_changed`, `assistant.asked`, `assistant.replied`:
none is a side effect and none carries `authz`; a send, an invite or a calendar write is an
outbox entry with its own `data.transfer`. Payloads carry ids, counts and enums, never a title,
a transcript line, a message body, an address, a link or an artifact label: an assistant message
points at the redacted raw-archive record (`message_ref`). Each is written in the world PE-12
names. `meeting.started` and `meeting.ended` gain optional `meeting_id` and, on `meeting.ended`,
`reason` (1.1.0, additive).

`world.share_changed` (registry 1.5.0, ADR-0040) is the one type of the world share: not a side effect, no `authz`, ids only, written in the shared world (PE-35).
