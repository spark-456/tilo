# Meeting room rules beyond the schema (meeting_room.schema.json 1.0.0, ADR-0028)

ADR-0028 is **Proposed**: these rules bind the builders once the user approves it. The schemas check
one record; the rules below need the world's layout, its other events, the team store or the archive.
tilo-api checks MR-01 to MR-05, MR-07, MR-08 and MR-12 before it writes an event (refusing the request on
any failure) and the room's reply step checks MR-06 and MR-11 before it writes a reply. MR-09 and MR-10
are the planner's and the run's. No validator runs them yet: ticket MR-A1 adds them to the api's checks
and MR-H2 to the plan validator (BQ-MROOM-9). Ids are stable; `MR-` here is not a merge request.

| Rule | Rule | Checked by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MR-01 | **Writers and envelope.** tilo-api writes every `meeting.*` type of ADR-0028 and the planner writes `plan.revised`, all through the world's one writer (EV-10); the web never writes one and a model never does. `authz` is null on all six (EV-05). `meeting.convened`, `meeting.asked` and `meeting.change_requested` have the convener (a user id) as `actor`; `meeting.replied` has the replying agent; `meeting.adjourned` has the convener for reason `person` and `system` otherwise; `plan.revised` has the world's Director. `run_id` of a `meeting.*` event is the run the room discusses; `plan.revised` with `run: revision_run` starts a new `run_id`. `vis` lists every attendee's district (the Director's room: `all`). | api; planner | 9.3, 9.4 HR-1, ADR-0028 |
| MR-02 | **Attendees.** `lead` and every `agents` entry are agents of the world's layout, `agents` never holds the lead, and the count is at most `config/meeting_room.yaml` `max_agents`. A district Lead brings only agents of its own district; the Director brings only district Leads, and only the world's owner may call the Director. | api | 8.1, 9.2 |
| MR-03 | **Who convenes, every time.** At each write of a room, not only at `meeting.convened`: the convener is the world's owner (`as_role: owner`), or an Approver or Member of the world's team (team store, BQ-DSN-01 labels) whose view of this world (PRD 10.1, the `vis` scopes they may read) covers every attendee's district. A Viewer never convenes. A failed check at a later turn adjourns the room with `access_lost`. No room opens on a paused world, and a world has at most `open_rooms_per_world` open rooms. | api | 10.1, FR-HIL-09, FR-TEAM-02 |
| MR-04 | **Turns.** A room's `meeting.asked` turns count 1, 2, ... with no gap, up to its `max_turns`. One turn is in flight: a new `meeting.asked` follows the previous turn's `meeting.replied`. Each `meeting.replied` answers the latest turn of its room once, with the turn's `to` as its actor and the turn's `mode`. | api | ADR-0028 |
| MR-05 | **Lifecycle.** Every room event follows its room's `meeting.convened` and precedes its `meeting.adjourned`; a room is adjourned at most once. Pause all and Stop all adjourn every open room of the world (`paused`) before `world.paused` is written. | api | FR-WLD-07, HR-1 |
| MR-06 | **Grounding.** A reply's `units` are units the replying agent ran (for a Lead: its district's units; for the Director: any unit) in the world's runs up to the discussed one, and its `artifacts` are versions those units made that the world recorded (EV-15). The reply step builds the model's context from those records, the approved brief and the agent's handoff notes only. If the model names any id outside that set, the reply is `failed` (nothing shown); ids are never silently dropped. | reply step | 9.4 HR-1, 16.3 |
| MR-07 | **A confirmed change is the shown proposal.** A `meeting.change_requested` names the `proposal_id` of an earlier `meeting.replied` of the same room with outcome `proposed_change`; its `units` and `artifacts` equal that proposal's and its `to` that reply's actor; its actor is the room's convener; the room is open; each proposal is confirmed at most once and a room confirms at most `max_changes` proposals. | api | ADR-0028 |
| MR-08 | **Room credits.** Before each reply call the room reserves the call's quote (MT-03); the sum of the room's `meeting.replied` credits never passes its `credit_cap`, and a turn whose quote would pass it is refused and adjourns the room (`capped`). The cap is also within the convener's remaining per-user cap (SEC-16, lowered by their team role's limit). `meeting.adjourned` `turns` is the room's number of `meeting.asked` and `credits_spent` the sum of its replies' credits. The run's budget cap pays for none of it. | api; meter | 14.1, FR-MTR-02, FR-MTR-04, SEC-16 |
| MR-09 | **Revision planning.** A `plan.revised` names the `proposal_id` of a `meeting.change_requested` of the same world, which is its `cause`. Each unit's `revises` is one of that change request's `units`, and the units are in the revised plan with `revises` and `change` set (PG-13). `run` is `same_run` when the discussed run had not finished at the change request and `revision_run` when it had; a `revision_run` event uses a `run_id` no earlier event of the world uses. | planner | ADR-0028, PG-13 |
| MR-10 | **Who pays for the revision.** A revision unit starts only (a) in a `same_run` revision confirmed by the world's owner whose estimate fits the run's remaining budget (MT-03), or (b) after a `budget.granted` that follows the `plan.revised`, with `authz.by` `person` and `authz.ref` the world's owner, never a policy. (b) is required for every `revision_run` and for every change confirmed by anyone but the owner. A `revision_run` then writes `world.started` with that cap. | run; validator | 10.1, 14.1, FR-MTR-03 |
| MR-11 | **Nothing in a room authorises.** No room event is a side effect. A `meeting.change_requested` is never an L2 instruction (OB-01 counts only `world.requested`). A proposal names units and artifact versions only; revision units get their actions, gates and risk tiers from the plan rules (PG-04, PG-06) and the policy engine, like any unit, and their side effects go through gates or policies and the outbox. | schema; planner; engine | 10.4, FR-HIL-03, FR-HIL-04 |
| MR-12 | **Text stays out of events.** The person's words, the replies and the confirmed wording are in the redacted raw archive (`message_ref`), written after the secret redactor; events carry refs only. The room's archive records are readable by the world's owner and the convener only, through the Scope; a district viewer sees that the room met, not what was said. | api; archive | 13.2, 16.5, MF-06 |
